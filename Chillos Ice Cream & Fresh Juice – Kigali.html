A FEW SWEET DETAILS

Good to know.

How do I place an order?

Choose a flavor, select the number of scoops or drink size, and add your selection to Your order. Add your pickup or delivery details, then continue to WhatsApp. Send the prepared message to Chillos. Based on your scoops, drink sizes, flavors, quantity, and delivery choice, Chillos will discuss and agree the final price with you on WhatsApp before confirming availability and timing. Adding items here does not place an order.

Can I get my ice cream delivered?

Choose delivery in your order and add your area or address. Our team will confirm coverage, timing, and any delivery fee on WhatsApp before you order.

What about allergies and dietary needs?

Please tell our team about any allergies before ordering. Ask about ingredients and possible cross-contact so we can help you choose.

How is my information used?

Your basket is saved in this browser until you remove its items or clear site data. This static website does not save customer details to a server. Your name, phone, address, and notes are passed to WhatsApp when you continue and are sent to Chillos when you send the message. Ask Chillos on 0781 281 062 about its message retention or deletion practices. WhatsApp’s privacy terms also apply.
