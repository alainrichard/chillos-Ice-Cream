FAQ

Good to know.

Can’t find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

How do I place an order?

Choose a treat, select your scoops or drink size, and add it to your order. Enter your pickup or delivery details, review the summary, then send the prepared message to us on WhatsApp. Our team will confirm the final price, availability and timing before anything is prepared. Adding items on this website does not place an order.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Choose delivery in your order and add your area or address. We will confirm coverage, timing and any delivery fee on WhatsApp.

What about allergies and dietary needs?

Please tell us about any allergies before ordering. Ask our team about ingredients and possible cross-contact so we can help you choose safely.

How is my information used?

Your basket is stored only in this browser. If you choose Remember my details, your name, phone, address and branch are also stored in this browser until you select Forget saved details; notes are never stored. This website does not send your details to any server. They are shared with Chillos only when you send the WhatsApp message, and WhatsApp’s privacy terms then apply.
