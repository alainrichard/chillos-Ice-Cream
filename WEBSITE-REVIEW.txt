OPEN THE SITE

FILES
index.html: storefront layout and content.
styles.css: responsive styling, separate from HTML.
catalog.js: product ideas, flavor choices and scoop/size options.
script.js: filtering, search, product customization, basket and WhatsApp handoff.
assets/generated/: 23 original generated images, compressed JPG website copies and PNG originals.
assets/generated/*PROMPTS.txt: exact image prompts and built-in generation-tool provenance.
check.cjs: functional checks. Run node check.cjs.
The original long HTML filename mirrors index.html. Keep both synchronized when editing.
When copying the site, include index.html, styles.css, catalog.js, script.js and assets.
